# Overtime Pay Calculator

## Descrição
Estimates weekly FLSA overtime for a covered nonexempt hourly employee.

## Funcionalidades
Regular-rate and overtime calculation, including nondiscretionary bonus allocation. Plus adds export.

## Experiência Essencial
Regular-rate and overtime calculation, including nondiscretionary bonus allocation.

## Prazzu Plus
Plus adds export.

## Regras
Federal overtime is generally at least 1.5x the regular rate after 40 hours; state law can be more protective.

## Dependências
Core Money/Percentage and integer rounding.
