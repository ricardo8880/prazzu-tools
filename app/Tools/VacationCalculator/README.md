# PTO Calculator

## Descrição
Estimates PTO balance, planned usage, projected accrual, and user-entered payout value.

## Funcionalidades
PTO balance and value estimate. Plus adds saved history and professional export.

## Experiência Essencial
PTO balance and value estimate.

## Prazzu Plus
Plus adds saved history and professional export.

## Regras
Federal law generally does not require paid vacation; accrual and payout depend on employer policy and state law.

## Dependências
Core Money/Percentage, history, persistence, and export services.

## Histórico de versões
- 2.1.0: Replaced Brazilian vacation-entitlement concepts with U.S. PTO assumptions.
