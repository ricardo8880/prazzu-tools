# PTO calculator assumptions

U.S. federal law does not create a general private-sector entitlement to paid vacation. PTO accrual, carryover, payout, and forfeiture rules depend on employer policy and applicable state/local law.

This tool therefore performs a policy-based mathematical estimate from the accrual rate, balance, hours, and pay rate entered by the user. It does not infer state-specific PTO obligations.
