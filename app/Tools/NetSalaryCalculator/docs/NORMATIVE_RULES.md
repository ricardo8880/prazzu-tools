# U.S. federal assumptions — 2026

This calculator is a general federal estimate for calendar year 2026. It is not a substitute for Form W-4 withholding tables, payroll software, or tax advice.

- Federal income tax: 2026 standard deductions and marginal brackets.
- Social Security: 6.2% employee rate up to the 2026 wage base of $184,500.
- Medicare: 1.45% employee rate with no wage base limit.
- Additional Medicare withholding: 0.9% on wages above the federal employer-withholding threshold used by the calculator.
- State and local withholding is entered separately because rules vary by jurisdiction.

Sources: IRS 2026 Publication 15/15-A and IRS 2026 inflation-adjustment guidance.
