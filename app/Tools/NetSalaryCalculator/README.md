# Take-Home Pay Calculator

## Descrição
Estimates monthly U.S. take-home pay using 2026 federal income-tax and FICA assumptions.

## Funcionalidades
Federal estimate plus user-entered state/local and benefit deductions. Plus adds variable earnings, custom deductions, history, and export.

## Experiência Essencial
Federal estimate plus user-entered state/local and benefit deductions.

## Prazzu Plus
Plus adds variable earnings, custom deductions, history, and export.

## Regras
Federal income tax is a simplified annual-liability estimate, not a Form W-4 payroll engine.

## Dependências
Core 2026 federal income-tax/payroll rules, history, and export services.
